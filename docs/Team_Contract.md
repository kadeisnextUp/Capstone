# Project
- Campus Companion
# Project Description
- Campus Companion will be an application geared towards first-year and transfer college students. Students will be able to upload their class schedule into the app, and it will show the student a map of their campus and the optimal routes for them to take. They will be able to select interests for clubs and campus events and be notified when there is an event that fits into their schedule. There will also be a section where students can rate and view ratings of professors to assist them in their scheduling process.
