# Constraints Essay: Campus Orientation App
**Team Members:** Kaden Sawyer, Laila Raines

For our project, we have decided to make it centered around helping first-year and transfer students get acclimated to college life. The application would show a map of the school, give the students route suggestions, and allow students to add their schedule. Based on their schedule, it would show different events being held on campus that match their interests.

## Legal
Because the app stores class schedules, we will follow FERPA's consent principles and require explicit student permission before saving any data. To avoid intellectual property issues, we will integrate an open map source instead of using the copyrighted school maps or logos. The trade-off is that consent screens add friction at sign-up, which puts pressure on our social goal of reaching as many new students as possible.

## Diversity and Cultural
Diversity and cultural factors shape our design because first-year and transfer students arrive with very different backgrounds and are unfamiliar with campus life. To serve them, the event feed will let students filter by interest and identity-based organizations rather than showing only the most popular events.

## Social
Being a first-year or transfer student can make it difficult to know about campus events. By having campus events on the student's schedule, it would lower the barriers for them and increase the likelihood of them participating in events. This is why the campus event recommendations would be a main feature on the application. The increased campus participation will help in making friendships and connections, improving their college experience.
